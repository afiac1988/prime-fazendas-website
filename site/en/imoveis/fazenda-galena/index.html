The property

A dual-purpose farm in Fortaleza do Tabocão, with 2,100 hectares (433 alqueires), 60% of which is cleared, fenced and divided with electric fencing. Part of the property has practiced crop-cattle rotation for over 10 years.

Mixed soil (sandy, gravelly and clay), rolling terrain, good water availability with streams, brooks, artesian wells and two ponds. Excellent logistics with direct access to the BR-153 highway.
