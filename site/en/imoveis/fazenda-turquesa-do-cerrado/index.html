The property

A 980-alqueire farm in the municipality of Novo Acordo-TO, 150km from Palmas and 1km from the paved road. Produces 1,700 hectares of cropland, with sandy soil and rich water resources from a large river and springs.

The rest of the land is used for cattle ranching, with average productivity of 60 sacks per hectare and annual rainfall of 1,600mm.
