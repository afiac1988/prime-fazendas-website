About Prime Fazendas

Specialists in farms and rural properties in the heart of Brazil.

Prime Fazendas is a real estate firm specialized in selling farms and rural properties, based in Tocantins — a strategic region with privileged logistics and one of the greatest growth potentials for agribusiness in the country.

With a qualified team and a strategic network of contacts, we offer an exclusive portfolio of farms, ranches and rural properties for investment, crop production, livestock, reforestation and leisure. Our experience in the sector lets us identify the best assets, ensuring security, transparency and appreciation potential for our clients.

The Prime Fazendas brand was formalized in 2025, but it carries over a decade of experience from founder André Amorim in the real estate market — the company is new, the track record in the sector is not.

Our story

Prime Fazendas was born from the journey of an entrepreneur who was always connected to the real estate market. At 18, André Amorim began his career as a real estate agent, standing out for his talent in negotiations and strategic vision in the sector.

With a restless spirit, he expanded his horizons and ventured into new businesses, always keeping a strong connection to real estate. It was then, at 24, that his journey took him to Tocantins, the heart of Brazil. The vastness of the farmland, the region's natural wealth and the potential of agribusiness made him see new opportunities — not just business ones, but a greater purpose.

The connection with the land grew naturally. Even his personal story reflects this bond: it was through a real estate deal that he met his wife, one more example of how real estate has always been a link of prosperity and connection in his life.

From this passion, Prime Fazendas was born — a real estate firm specialized in selling farms, with a clear commitment: to offer solid, strategic opportunities for investors and rural producers, always with excellence and respect for nature.

Our difference

Acquiring a rural property goes far beyond buying land — it's the expansion of an estate and the start of new opportunities. That's why we offer complete, personalized consulting, from prospecting to closing the deal, with legal advisory and technical support.

Mission

To transform the experience of buying and selling farms into a fast, secure and profitable process, promoting the development of agribusiness and contributing to the sustainable growth of the sector. We seek not only to close deals, but to build long-term relationships based on trust, ethics and commitment to our clients' satisfaction.

Values

  • Transparency and ethics in every deal
  • Commitment to sustainability and legality
  • Long-term relationships over transactions
  • Respect for the land, for nature, and for those who live off it
