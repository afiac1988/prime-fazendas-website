Frequently asked questions

Common questions about selling and structuring a Data Center

Does Prime Fazendas sell Data Centers?

Yes. We sell land and assets already structured for Data Centers, and we also structure deals from scratch — from site selection to the power contract — for Brazilian and foreign investors.

What does "structuring" a Data Center mean?

It means organizing everything that makes the project viable before construction begins: land with clean documentation, a power contract (including renewable energy), water rights where applicable, environmental licensing, and the corporate/financial structure of the deal.

Do you manage the Data Center construction?

Yes. We coordinate construction with engineering partners specialized in critical infrastructure, from earthworks through delivery of the building ready to receive racks.

What Data Center sizes does Prime Fazendas offer?

We work with three reference models — MicroCenter (up to 1 MW), DataCenter Standard (up to 5 MW) and HyperScale (20 MW or more) — plus custom projects sized to the client's specific demand.

Do you work with investors outside Brazil?

Yes. Much of the demand for Data Centers in Brazil comes from international players. We support compliance, documentation and communication in both Portuguese and English.

Why build a Data Center in Brazil?

Brazil has a predominantly renewable power grid, competitively priced available land, climate stability and a strategic geographic position between the Americas — factors that reduce cost and operational risk.
